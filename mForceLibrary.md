## Feature Roadmap: Information Mining

### 1. The Curator Terminal
A fluid, context-aware interaction model where the terminal serves as the heartbeat of the system.
* **Dynamic Docking:** The terminal starts centered for focus and animates to the bottom-left "workspace" layout once interaction begins.
* **Inverted Type-Ahead:** Search suggestions render above the input field to maintain visibility in the docked state.

### 2. The Information Mining Interface
* **Inline Expansion:** Search results expand in place (accordion style) to reveal sub-modules without immediate navigation.
* **Context Pathways:** A 3-column grid showing Provenance (Left), Focus (Center), and Associative/Related topics (Right).
* **Contextual Breadcrumbs:** A visual history of the user's thought process (e.g., `[Search: "Auth"] -> [Expanded: "Tokens"]`).

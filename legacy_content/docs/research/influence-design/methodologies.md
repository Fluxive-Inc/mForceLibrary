# Operational Methodologies & Playbooks

## The Influence Architecture Workflow

A 7-step blueprint for building human-machine collaboration:

1.  **Capture As-Is State**: Map every process step, input, output, and decision point. Validate roles and responsibilities.
2.  **Identify AI-Enablement Points**: Locate bottlenecks, repetitive tasks, or decision points where data analysis can guide human choice.
3.  **Single Out Tasks**: Clearly allocate specific functions to either humans or AI to avoid role duplication and ambiguity.
4.  **Define Human-AI Interaction**: Design the interface—how users input data and how they receive/process AI outputs.
5.  **Define Triggers & Events**: Establish time-based (e.g., monthly) or data-driven (e.g., threshold alerts) triggers for AI intervention.
6.  **Feedback Loops**: Create mechanisms to evaluate performance, enabling the algorithm to learn and the human to provide course correction.
7.  **Embrace Evolution**: Continuously refactor the blueprint. It is not static; it must evolve with organizational growth.

## The Insight Compass

A visual tool embedded in dashboards to ensure data literacy and transparency.

*   **The Problem**: Complex visualizations can be misleading without context.
*   **The Solution**: A "Compass" (e.g., a gauge, bullseye, or status bar) that instantly tells the user the maturity level of the data:
    *   **Descriptive** (Past)
    *   **Diagnostic** (Why)
    *   **Predictive** (Future)
    *   **Prescriptive** (Action)
*   **Benefit**: Prevents users from mistaking a raw data report for a prescriptive recommendation.

## One-Sheet Discussion Frameworks

A methodology to fix the "Thin Wide Line" disconnect between business and tech teams.

### Format
*   **Front**: The main story + 3 provocative, high-level questions.
*   **Back**: Teaser or supporting data.

### Principles
*   **Brevity**: Must fit on one sheet to force focus.
*   **Visuals**: Use graphics/illustrations, as they are easier to recall than text.
*   **Interactive**: Participants should write, draw, and circle items on the sheet during the meeting.

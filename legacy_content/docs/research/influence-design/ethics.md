# Ethics, Risk, and Governance

## Expanded Model Risk Management (MRM)

MRM must evolve from a banking-specific compliance task to a strategic organizational function.

### New Categories for AI
*   **Alignment AI**: Tools that standardize and measure.
*   **Investigative AI**: Tools for exploration and understanding.
*   **Influential AI**: Tools for real-time interaction and decision support.

**Documentation Requirements**: MRM must document the "Data Scope & Freshness," "Expected Decision Patterns," "Audience Exposure," and "Expected Lifespan" of every model.

---

## Principles of Ethical Influence

To prevent "dark patterns" and manipulation, AI influence must adhere to strict ethical guidelines.

1.  **Transparency**: Users must know when AI is shaping their experience.
2.  **User Control**: Users must have the ability to opt-out of recommendations.
3.  **Human Oversight**: A human must continuously monitor AI decision-making to ensure alignment with human values.

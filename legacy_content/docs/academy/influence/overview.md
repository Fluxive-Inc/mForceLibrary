# Influence Architecture

> "AI is an engine; purpose is the driver."

**Influence Architecture** is the business-first discipline for managing Artificial Intelligence. Unlike technical governance, which focuses on code execution and uptime, Influence Architecture focuses on **behavioral alignment**.

## The Exponential AI Risk Gap

As AI capabilities grow exponentially, a business's ability to manage the consequences of AI actions often lags behind. This creates the **Exponential AI Risk Gap**.

Influence Architecture bridges this gap by shifting focus from "Prompt Engineering" to **"Intent Alignment"**.

## Core Trinity

Effective Influence Architecture relies on the "Holy Trinity of Information Management":

1.  **Data**: The raw information available to the system.
2.  **Context**: The nuance and human understanding required to interpret that data correctly.
3.  **Intent**: The strategic goal or purpose driving the AI's actions.

## Moving Beyond IT

Traditionally, technology governance belongs to IT. However, because AI agents make *decisions* that impact brand, legal liability, and customer experience, governance must move to the **C-Suite**.

*   **Technical Governance**: Is the server up? Is the code secure? (IT Responsibility)
*   **Influence Governance**: Is the AI acting in accordance with our values? (Business Leadership Responsibility)

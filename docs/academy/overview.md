# The Academy

Master the art of **Influence Architecture**.

## Certification Path

**Becoming an Influence Architect**

This certification path is designed for business leaders, strategists, and advanced technologists who want to lead the Machineforce.

### Curriculum
1.  **Foundations of Influence**: Understanding Context, Data, and Intent.
2.  **Machineforce Management**: Taxonomy, auditing, and steering agents.
3.  **Strategic Deployment**: Designing Influence Nodes and feedback loops.

## Glossary

*   **Influence Design**: The practice of shaping AI behavior through context and intent alignment.
*   **Machineforce**: The collective body of AI agents in an organization.
*   **Intent Alignment**: The degree to which an AI's actions match the strategic goals of the business.
*   **fxPersistence**: Fluxive's proprietary memory handling layer.
*   **Influence Node**: A specific point in a workflow where human leadership intersects with AI execution.
